## ✨ What is Daily Habits Tracking?

**Daily Habits Tracking** is your personal training companion 🏋️‍♂️🏋️‍♀️.
Plan your workouts, tick off daily habits, track your steps and water, and see your progress
by day, week and month — all with a bold black-and-yellow gym look. 🟨⬛

> 🔒 **Your data stays with you.** Everything is saved on your own device (browser storage). No account, no server, no tracking.

---


## 🚀 Features

- 📅 **Day view** — a daily sheet with your workout, exercises, and a live **Day Score**
- 🏋️ **Workout planner** — add exercises, reuse your previous workout, rename each weekday (e.g. *"Saturday: Cardio and Core"*)
- 📆 **Week & Month views** — workouts done, habit %, routine %, steps, drinks and overall score
- ✅ **Habit tracker** — wake up at 5 AM, drink 6 L of water, read, meditate, journal, plan tomorrow...
- 👟 **Step goal** — quick-add buttons (+500 / +1,000 / +2,500) with a daily goal (default 10,000)
- 💧 **Drink logger** — log water and other drinks in ml
- 🧘 **Affirmation** — your own *"I am..."* statement shown every day
- 📄 **PDF reports** — download a **Day Sheet** or a full report for *today / this week / this month / last 30 days / all time*
- 🖼️ **Graphs PDF button** — one tap downloads a graphs-only PDF: weekly, monthly and yearly pages, each with a **bar, line, pie and column graph**
- 📊 **PDF graphs** — every report has weekly, monthly and yearly pages, each with a **bar, line, pie and column graph** (water, day score, steps share and steps)
- 💾 **Backup by period** — in Setup, back up the last 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks, or 1, 3, 6 or 12 months (.json or .csv). Restoring a period backup merges those days into your data
- 📴 **Works offline** — it's a plain static site, just open it
- 🎨 **Bold gym design** — hazard-stripe theme, dark mode, built for thumbs 👍

## ▶️ Run It

**Option 1 — just open it** 😎
Double-click `index.html`.

**Option 2 — local server**
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then visit http://localhost:8000
```

**Option 3 — on your phone** 📱
Open the hosted link (GitHub Pages, below) in Chrome/Safari and tap
**Share → Add to Home Screen** for an app-like experience.
